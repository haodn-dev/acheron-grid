Keyboard and selection

Find or Ctrl/Cmd+F searches values. Enter/Shift+Enter moves between matches; Escape closes search. Click or use arrows to select. Shift extends a range; Ctrl/Cmd+click adds another. Shift+F8 arms the next click/navigation to add a range. Escape clears selection. Copy/paste requires one range. Click Approved to toggle its checkbox; F2/Space/Enter also edits it. Enter/F2 edits Name or Status; Long text expands the editor. Enter saves; Alt/Ctrl/Cmd+Enter inserts a newline; Tab saves and moves; Escape cancels. Invalid input shows an error beside the editor. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redoes. Right-click for Lock/Unlock cell, row, column or table, Freeze/Unfreeze and resizing. Locks prevent value edits while preserving selection/copy. Frozen controls preserve selection and undo history.

Appearance and Reset view remount the grid: selection and undo history reset; edited sample values remain. Canvas cells do not yet have a complete screen-reader grid tree.
